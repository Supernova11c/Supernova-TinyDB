Persistent by design

Store records on the server, not in a browser tab. Your data is still there after refresh and sign-out.

⌑

Project isolation

Every query is scoped to an authenticated project. One project's credentials cannot read another project's records.

Public keys are read-only by default. Choose collection-level insert, update, and delete permissions.
